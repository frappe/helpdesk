# The interview

Ask in this order. Most people answer all of it in about 30 minutes. Take answers from call notes when you have them, and ask only for what is missing. Write down their words: the plan uses them.

Each answer turns steps in `steps.md` on or off, or fills in a value the step needs.

| # | Ask | What the answer decides |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | In a normal week, what do you lose track of: customer issues and requests, new prospects, or orders? | **Fit.** Customer issues: carry on. Prospects: say CRM should come first. Orders and stock: say ERPNext should come first. |
| 1 | What does "Helpdesk is working" look like for you a month from now? | Their live condition. It must be something you can check on data, not a date. |
| 2 | By when do you need it working? | The pace of the plan. |
| 3 | Where do customer issues reach you today: email, phone, WhatsApp, a form, in person? | Email: `inbox`. A web form: `portal`. Phone, WhatsApp or chat apps: note as an ask; agents log those tickets by hand for now. |
| 4 | About how many issues a week, and how many people answer them? | `agents`, and the numbers in `regular-use`. |
| 5 | Who picks up a new issue today? Do different people handle different kinds? | `assignment`. Separate groups: `teams`. |
| 6 | What kinds of issues do you get? Which ones are urgent? | `ticket-types`, and the priorities. |
| 7 | Do you promise anyone a response or resolution time? What are your working hours and holidays? | `sla`. |
| 8 | What do you write again and again? | `saved-replies`. |
| 9 | Are your customers companies with several people, or individuals? Do you have a list? | `customers`. |
| 10 | Do you have FAQs or help pages today? | `knowledge-base`. |
| 11 | Should customers log in to raise and follow their own tickets? | `portal`. |
| 12 | Do you want customers to rate how you did? | `feedback`. |
| 13 | What name and logo should customers see? | `basics`. |

## Asking well

- Ask one or two questions at a time, in plain words. Do not show this table.
- When an answer names a number, a name or a list, read it back.
- When they do not know, write "not known yet" and move on. Do not guess for them.
- A promise they cannot keep today is not an SLA yet. Set the times they actually meet, and raise them later.
